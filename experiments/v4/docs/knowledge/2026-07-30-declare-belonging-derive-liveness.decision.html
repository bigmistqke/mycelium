What deciduous #310 calls the missing retirement concept is not a property of each node: v0's decisions are dormant because the v0 exploration was abandoned — one fact denormalized across 200 nodes, and the next abandonment costs another 200 stamps.

Split what status conflates. Belonging (which exploration a node is part of) never changes and can be multi-valued.
