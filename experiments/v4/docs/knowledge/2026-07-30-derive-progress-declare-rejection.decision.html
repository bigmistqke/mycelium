Derive progress from steps; keep rejected declared, because abandonment is an act 85 completed

The four status values on plan-task and plan-doc are not the same kind of thing, and that is why storing all four rots.

Three are progress, and progress is a fold over the steps:

  • every step completed → completed
  • some completed → active
  • none → pending

Nothing about those requires a human. Storing them creates a second source of truth that can disagree with the first, and the disagreement is silent.

One is not derivable. rejected means somebody decided to abandon the work. No arrangement of steps implies it — a task with zero completed steps is pending if you still intend to do it and rejected if you don't, and the difference lives only in someone's head until they write it down.

So the field survives, narrowed to the part that cannot be computed. plan-status becomes optional on doc and task, and its only legal value is rejected. Present means abandoned; absent means read the steps. A field that can only say the one thing the layer below cannot is the residue after derivation, not metadata.

The same shape as the belonging/liveness split (that decision), and it survives the objection that sank the tag half of it: this declaration is not recoverable from anywhere else, where belonging was already recoverable from the filename and the edges.

plan-step is untouched. Its two values are already the layer everything else folds from, and check sets them from real command exit codes rather than from anyone's memory.
