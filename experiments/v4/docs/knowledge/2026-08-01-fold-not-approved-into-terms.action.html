Fold not-approved words into the term that replaces them 85 completed

Six words had their own entries saying only Not used here and pointing at a replacement. None was a definition. Each cost a document, an edge, and a required language-meaning field it had nothing to put in.

They are now a comma-separated language-avoid field on the word that replaces them, so use carries utilize, leverage. language-status is deleted with them: a term is approved by existing, which is the third declared field today that turned out to say something the structure already said.

What this gives up is the lookup ASD-STE100 is built around. Its dictionary is one alphabetical list holding both kinds, so you look up the word you wrote rather than the word you should have written. Here there is no longer a file named for a word you should not use, and the checker's message has to carry that instead. Chosen deliberately after weighing it; browsing a directory of only approved words was worth more than the reverse lookup.

Two links broke while doing it, both inside the entries being merged: use named utilize and leverage in its own meaning, and both files had just been deleted. Nothing caught it, which is what prompted the link audit committed alongside.
