140 sentences bury a clause between two dashes. The guide describing the habit is sharpened to name the paired form precisely, and stays a guide until the corpus follows it.

Unlike the three migrations done alongside it, this one is not mechanical. Renaming a path, deleting a footer and unwrapping a tag all leave meaning untouched. Recasting The middle layers — architecture, component — earn their place means rewriting the sentence, and 57 of the 140 sit inside knowledge nodes. Substituting a word in a node preserves what somebody wrote. Lifting a buried clause out changes emphasis and structure, which is closer to editing how they argued, and is the same line drawn around verbatim prompts and quotations one step softer.

Most follow one shape, X — apposition — verb, and turning that into X, apposition, verb is usually safe. That is a starting point rather than a plan: the ones that do not fit are the ones worth attention, and a pass that batches the easy cases and skims the rest is how a style rule stops improving prose and starts flattening it.
