LLMs are trained on code syntax, not dataflow graphs, so graph work may fight the training 82

Imported from the project’s earlier decision log, frozen 2026-07-23.

Training mismatch: LLMs are trained on billions of lines of code syntax — if/else, loops, functions. Very few dataflow graphs appear in that training data.

The AI's intuition for code patterns is deep. Asking it to work in graphs may fight the training rather than use it — the AI might perform worse on dataflow than on code.
