Linking is not composing. Every family here can hyperlink, and a hyperlink points: it makes no part of one document's meaning depend on another's. A shared vocabulary is the unit that does compose, and there is nowhere to put one.

The second half is the voice — the language used across every document rather than the structure of any one of them. This project has templated the shape of every family and left the sentences entirely unconstrained, which is where the value sits.

Settled during the discussion: the family is real (a template creates the dictionary), the enforcement is an audit rather than a template, and the model is ASD-STE100's own two-part shape — writing rules plus a dictionary — with our dictionaries rather than its aviation one.
