Stylesheets come from the template, and not every need becomes a field 85 completed

Imported from the project’s earlier decision log, frozen 2026-07-23. Two related corrections in one pass.

1. Stylesheets. Every v4 document duplicated an identical inline style block. Split into theme.css (shared base, linked by every document) plus spec.template.css (the spec-* family's own rendering rules, sibling to spec.template.html, later mirrored by knowledge.template.css).

This does not weaken DESIGN.html's self-containment argument, which is about data never living elsewhere, not about zero external resources — DESIGN.html already loads an external mermaid script.

2. Field versus link, a principle left implicit after the header-versus-sections correction. A template field earns its place when the same question should be askable across every instance of a type without opening each one by hand. A one-off reference stays a plain data-rel link — inside a section, or outside the structured element entirely — and is no less queryable, just queryable as a relationship rather than as a uniform field.

This guards against the field list growing without bound as further template families get built.
