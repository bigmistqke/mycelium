Rejected: audit.template.html as its own family 80

Give audits a family file of their own, the way knowledge, spec, plan and dictionary each have one. Argued for on the grounds that family resolution is by filename, so audit.template.html would yield mycelium run audit list for free, in the shape every other family already has.

Rejected. That command is not needed: the roster is the visibility fix, and the roster reads audits directly. And a family whose instances live scattered inside other template files is unlike every family here, all of which keep their instances in a directory of their own. The separate file was solving a naming problem that a better reading of the naming rule dissolves.
