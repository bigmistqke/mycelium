Curry-Howard is the mechanism behind 'axiom as type': the type-checker is the proof-checker 70 explains the mechanism behind the Curry-Howard-style proof-term family this observation proposes raises the question of where this mechanism stops applying

The Curry-Howard correspondence is the mechanism behind “axiom as type”:

  • a proposition corresponds to a type;
  • a proof of that proposition corresponds to a program inhabiting that type.

So a dependently-typed language's ordinary type-checker — already checking that code compiles — is simultaneously checking that a proof is valid. No separate proof-checking step is required.
