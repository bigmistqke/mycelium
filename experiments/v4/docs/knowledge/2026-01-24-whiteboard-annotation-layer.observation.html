Imported from the project’s earlier decision log, frozen 2026-07-23.

An annotation layer floats on top of the auto-generated visualization. The human can select nodes, add comments, and draw.

Annotations don’t fight the layout: they anchor to nodes, or become orphaned if the structure changes.

 annotations must anchor to nodes precisely because the layout is generated, not hand-arranged
