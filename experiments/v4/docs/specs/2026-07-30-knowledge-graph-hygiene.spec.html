Unify the knowledge graph and replace the rate gate with shape audits draft 2026-07-30

Two corpora hold this project's decision history, and both are degraded in the same way for different reasons. Deciduous holds 400 SQLite nodes, frozen 2026-07-23. The v4 HTML graph holds 137 nodes and is live. Neither can be read as a narrative, which is what a knowledge graph is for.

Measured on the v4 graph, 2026-07-30:

  • 65 of 137 nodes (47%) are action; 53 of those carry a commit hash, so their content is what the commit message already says.
  • option 0, contradicts 0, blocks 0. The graph cannot express a rejected alternative or a reversal.
  • Edge labels resolves and produced are used once each and both duplicate leads_to.
  • The 2026-07-27 batch averages 415 characters per title against 117 for the rest of the graph, and uses knowledge-detail zero times in eight nodes.
  • 77 of 137 nodes predate knowledge-detail (added 4738449, 2026-07-24 15:19). Of the 52 written after it existed, 12 use it.

Measured on deciduous: 400 nodes, 463 edges, zero descriptions — the field exists and was never populated, so the entire finding sits in title, averaging 616 characters across the July 2026 material. 361 of 400 sit at status pending. The edge vocabulary collapsed to leads_to (448), chosen (11), rejected (4).

The cause is the harness, not editorial judgment.

  • require-action-node.sh blocks every Edit and Write unless a .goal.html or .action.html was touched in the last 15 minutes. That is a rate, not an event: a long session must emit an action node every quarter hour to keep working. This is the 47%.
  • Neither that hook's guidance block nor CLAUDE.md's add and update signatures mention --detail, though the CLI has supported it on both since 4738449. detail appears in CLAUDE.md once, in prose, as a parenthetical. An agent copies the signature, not the prose. This is the title-cramming.
  • post-commit-reminder.sh prompts a node per commit, which stop-per-commit-nodes.decision.html decided against. Its text also still claims the CLI cannot update existing nodes, which update fixed.
  • /decision documents a five-node-per-feature chain (goal to decision to option to action to outcome) with one-line titles — the exact shape of deciduous's 195-node January feature grind. It and /work and /recover are all allowed-tools: Bash(deciduous:*), driving the frozen CLI; /recover is labelled USE THIS ON SESSION START. Seven of eight project slash commands target frozen or doomed systems.

This inverts deciduous #349. That node established the project's founding result: discipline fails (52% compliance with a bolded CLAUDE.md instruction) and protocols work (near-total compliance with a hook). v4 built the protocol, and the protocol works — which is the problem. It enforces "emit an action node every 15 minutes" at close to 100%. The corollary never logged: a protocol that works enforces whatever it encodes, including the clutter. A time-based trigger encodes "log at a rate" when the goal is "log when there is something to say."

Three parts, in dependency order. The harness changes first, because the audits are what identify what to prune, and because a migration into an unfixed harness refills with clutter at one action per 15 minutes.

Part 1 — Shape audits replace the rate gate

The 15-minute timer gates on quantity: did you log? Every measured failure is quality: is what you logged shaped right? Quantity is what a timer can measure, which is why the harness measures it.

The good triggers — a rejected alternative, a contradicted prediction, a correction — are not machine-detectable. No hook can tell that something was learned. But the failures are all machine-detectable, and that is enough.

  • title-length — a title beyond roughly 140 characters is a paragraph in the wrong field. Measured 2026-07-30: this flags 47 of 149 nodes, not the 8 first assumed. The 2026-07-27 batch is the smallest contributor (11 from 07-23, 4 from 07-24, 24 from 07-25, 8 from 07-27), so title-cramming is a graph-wide habit, not one bad night. There is also no knee in the distribution to calibrate against — median 84, p90 302, and the count falls smoothly (>100: 61, >140: 47, >200: 28, >300: 17). 140 is a judgment call, not a discovered boundary.
  • hollow-action — an action with neither detail nor commit is either redundant with git or empty.
  • decision-without-alternative — cut 2026-07-30. A decision with no option and no alternative_to edge does record a choice without a choosing, and it measured 17 of 21. It is removed anyway, because under a failing gate an audit must be repairable and this one is not: it asks what was rejected months ago, which was frequently never written down. The only ways to go green would be to invent alternatives or to suppress the audit — the first is worse than the gap, the second is the decay this spec exists to prevent. An audit nobody can satisfy honestly teaches that audits get bypassed.

So two audits, not three. Both are repairable from material that already exists, which is what makes failing the build a real gate rather than a permanent red. Scope: 52 distinct nodes of 149.

These are data-audits scripts collocated in knowledge.template.html beside the two that already live there, discovered and run by the existing crawler. No new machinery.

require-action-node.sh and post-commit-reminder.sh are both deleted. v3's lesson survives intact: this is still a protocol rather than discipline, gating on shape instead of rate. It cannot make an agent notice something worth logging — nothing can — but it stops the two failure modes that are measurable.

Part 2 — A vocabulary that can hold a story

  • --detail enters every documented signature: CLAUDE.md's add and update, and any hook or skill guidance text. This single omission is the whole 0-of-8.
  • contradicts is taught and used. It exists in the vocabulary and has never fired, while the July 2026 deciduous record is built from reversals — four documented self-corrections in one session.
  • option is taught. Zero in v4, 48 in deciduous; the only skill teaching it points at the dead CLI.
  • resolves and produced fold into leads_to.
  • The dead slash commands are removed: /decision and /work (frozen CLI, and /decision teaches the grind), plus the v0 and v2 commands.
  • /recover is replaced, not just removed. It drives the same frozen CLI, but it is the only thing that ever read the graph back, and deleting it in the pass that adds write pressure gets the incentive backwards — the design-rationale literature's failure mode is that the writer is not the beneficiary. A knowledge recover command surfaces the live threads (active goals, decisions with no outcome, contested claims) and runs at SessionStart. Deletion and replacement ship together, so the read path is never absent.

Part 3 — Unify, with belonging declared and liveness derived

A status enum assumes linear history with a single endpoint. What deciduous node #5 calls the missing "retirement" concept is not a property of each node at all — v0's decisions are dormant because the v0 exploration was abandoned, one fact denormalized across 200 nodes. Adding a fifth enum value is exactly the hand-maintained metadata that #364 predicts will rot.

Split what status conflates:

  • Belonging — which exploration a node is part of. Never changes, can be multi-valued. Declared, as a topic tag.
  • Liveness — whether it still holds. Changes because of events outside the node. Derived from topology: one v0-abandoned node with edges to what it supersedes, and an audit computes reachability.

Subdirectories are rejected despite the navigability gain. They cost a rewrite of every ./<node>.html, ../theme.css and ../templates/ reference across roughly 320 files; they break inbound links whenever a node moves; and they force exclusive membership, when v0's query-interface decision belongs both to v0 and to the v3 synthesis that leans on it. A directory hard-codes one grouping; tags let every grouping be derived, including a generated index. Grouping is a query, not a mkdir.

The migration cut criterion: keep what git cannot tell you — evidence, rejection, reversal. Git already records what changed, when, and a one-line why; it cannot record what was rejected, what turned out wrong, or what evidence was gathered.

The criterion selects, it does not decide. All 400 nodes were read on 2026-07-30 and the cut is now made per node, not by applying the rule mechanically to reach a number. The earlier "roughly 183" estimate is withdrawn: it was derived before anyone had read the corpus, and reading it showed the rule alone keeps too much. Every decision plus its option children passes — which covers #2–#5 (TypeScript vs Rust vs Hybrid) and #43/#44 (package manager → pnpm) equally, though only one of those ever mattered. The missing test is did the choice have consequences, and no rule supplies it.

Two constraints bound the per-node review. The transitive closure over citations still holds, so no surviving node is left with a dangling reference such as #270. And the v4 graph already cites #5, #20, #325, #349, #350, #351 and #364, so those and their closure are kept regardless of the review — otherwise the graph cannot resolve its own citations, which is the concrete argument for doing the import at all.

Long titles split into a real title plus detail — the same repair on both corpora, since the 2026-07-27 batch has deciduous's shape. Inline citations such as #270 and #349 become data-rel edges.
