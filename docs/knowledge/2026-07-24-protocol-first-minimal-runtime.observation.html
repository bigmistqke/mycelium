A protocol of a few attributes, not a bespoke serialization, keeps the runtime minimal 75 the data-side consequence of the fusion: a thin protocol keeps the engine that reads it thin too

The data side of that fusion is just a handful of attributes and one script-type convention — data-conforms-to, data-validates, type="mycelium/command" — not a bespoke serialization.

Because of that, the runtime needed to interpret it stays minimal and easily reimplementable: run.ts/validate.ts only ever need to know the protocol, never what any template family means.
