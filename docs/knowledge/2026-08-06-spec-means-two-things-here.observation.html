The chain's second rung is a specification in the sense the W3C means: a standing normative document, revised in place, whose requirements carry permanent addresses that other work cites.

This repository already spent the word on something else. spec.template.html states its purpose as the shape a design/brainstorming document takes, overriding one skill's Markdown default, and its instances carry a date prefix because each one belongs to the exploration that produced it.

The two differ on every property the chain leans on. A design document records what someone thought in July and stays that way. A specification says what the system does now, and an edit to it is a change to the requirement rather than a note about the past. One is dated by nature and the other cannot be, which is the same split language.template.html already argued when it dropped the date prefix its siblings all carry.
