Behaviour depth ranks into columns, the way axiom depth already does 85 active

A narrowing behaviour sits one column right of the behaviour it narrows. The chain page already ranks axioms this way, filtering each depth into its own column, so behaviours reuse the machinery rather than growing a second idea about how a rank works.

Nesting boxes inside boxes was the alternative. It keeps a subsystem together and makes the widths uneven, and it would mean two kinds of nesting on one page for one relation.

The cost is real and worth naming: a deep subsystem spreads across more columns, and the page grows sideways. The same cost already applies to axioms and nobody has minded.
