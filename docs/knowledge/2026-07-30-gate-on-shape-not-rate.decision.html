The 15-minute timer gates on quantity — did you log? — because quantity is what a timer can measure. Every measured failure is quality: is what you logged shaped right?

The good triggers (a rejected alternative, a contradicted prediction, a correction) are not machine-detectable; no hook can tell that something was learned. But the failure modes all are, and that is enough. Three data-audits scripts collocated in knowledge.template.html, run by the existing crawler, no new machinery: title-length, hollow-action, decision-without-alternative.
