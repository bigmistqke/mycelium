Probe whether headless Chrome fires requestAnimationFrame and measures a figure's grid 80 completed

Every browser-test of the figure engine rests on one assumption nobody has checked: that headless Chrome runs the same layout a visible one does. figure.element.js draws inside a requestAnimationFrame callback and redraws from a ResizeObserver, and it reads every coordinate from getBoundingClientRect.

The probe needs no driver. The engine writes its measurements into the document as an SVG path's d attribute, so a dump of the DOM after load separates the two answers on its own. Zero rects give M0,0 L0,0 L0,0 L0,0, the same shape happy-dom produced. Real layout gives real coordinates.

This is the same probe that settled the happy-dom question, pointed at the other candidate venue.
