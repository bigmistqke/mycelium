A specification carries no prose, because every other kind of content has a family 85 completed

A specification is a title, edges up to the axioms it serves, and a list of behaviours. No prose field, and no detail field either, though every other type in this corpus has one.

What forces this: each kind of content that is not a behaviour already has a family. A criterion is an axiom. Something that happened is a knowledge observation or decision. A word's meaning is a language term, and the reasoning behind a design is the design document.

Two examples that looked like prose and were not. This gate only became real on 2026-07-30; before that it printed failures and exited 0 is an incident and belongs in the graph. It exits non-zero rather than warning because a gate people ignore is not a gate glues a behaviour to its criterion, and the halves separate into a behaviour and an axiom.

A detail field is the hole both of those go through. The cost is that a specification reads as a list rather than an explanation, so a reader who wants to know why the subsystem took this shape reads the axioms above it and the design document beside it.
