565 nodes in one force layout says almost nothing. The drawing settles into a shape nobody chose, and reading it means untangling rather than following.

Knowledge moves through time, and every node carries its day in its filename, so the axis the drawing needs already exists and nothing uses it.

Counted before designing: 502 of 629 edges join two nodes from the same day, and 127 cross days. A day is therefore a partition rather than a rank — making each day a column would leave four fifths of the edges inside one column, which is the fault the chain page exists to avoid.

That gives two levels rather than one. Days run left to right, and a day's own nodes rank into sub-columns inside it, which is the shape the chain already draws well.
