Graph manipulation interface: instead of the AI reading and writing full JSON, the AI issues commands — add_node(), wire(), delete(). The tool maintains graph state; the AI speaks in verbs, not nouns.

Solves context bloat (small commands rather than a full dump) and the training mismatch (“wire X to Y” is natural language).
