Counted across every node: 570 values, a median of 85, and 342 of them are 80, 85 or 90. The range runs 20 to 95, so nothing ever expressed real doubt either.

Nothing consumes the number. The only files naming knowledge-confidence are the stylesheet that renders it, the template that declares it, and the generic validator that checks it is a number in range. No audit reads it, no command sorts by it, and the drawing ignores it.

The canon reached this conclusion already and acted on it. An axiom carried a confidence meant to separate a principle somebody committed to from one an agent inferred, eleven values sat between 80 and 95, nothing read them, and not one ever moved. The field went.

The difference here is scale rather than kind: the same field, required on all six types, filled 570 times, still unread. Removing it is a schema change against every node in the corpus, which is what makes it worth deciding deliberately rather than in passing.
