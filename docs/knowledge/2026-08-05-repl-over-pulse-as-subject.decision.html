Analyse repl rather than pulse, because repl's author is an answer key the artefacts do not descend from 84 completed

pulse fails as a subject for one reason. An agent wrote its documents, and the agent writing its commit messages had those documents in view, so every channel available to a reader descends from one source. Agreement between a report and that answer key would be partly inherited rather than earned. That is the same defect the three-readings pass ran into.

repl is 221 commits across two years, 3,449 lines over 33 files, and 1,443 words of prose, all of it one README. One human author wrote all of it.

So it has no answer key on paper at all, and its author is the answer key instead. That memory does not descend from the commits, which is the property pulse could not offer.

Two further properties. The commit histogram has real gaps, with nothing between 2024-09 and 2025-01 and nothing between 2025-03 and 2025-06, so era boundaries can be checked against what someone remembers doing. And the project converged on something simple, meaning its abandoned complexity survives in the history and nowhere else — the case pulse could not test, having written everything down.

The cost is that no scored diff falls out. The grade is a conversation. The earlier pass suggests the score was never the informative part anyway.
