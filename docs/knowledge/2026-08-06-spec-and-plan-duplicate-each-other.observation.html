A spec and its plan state the same design twice and pair only by filename 90

Nineteen specs and eleven plans sit in this corpus, and ten of the eleven plans have a spec carrying the same date and nearly the same slug. Nothing links a pair. The relation lives in the filename, where no audit reaches it.

The two documents overlap in what they require, not only in when they appear. spec-doc requires spec-design. plan-doc requires plan-goal and plan-architecture. So one piece of work states its design twice, in two files, and nothing keeps the two readings in step.

Status doubles up as well. spec-status takes draft, approved or implemented, while a plan's status folds up from its steps and the exit codes their checks return. Implemented and every-step-complete are the same claim, one of them asserted and the other derived.

Three duplications for a pair of documents that arrive together and describe one piece of work.
