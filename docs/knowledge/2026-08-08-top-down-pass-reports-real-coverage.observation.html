The first top-down pass reported seventeen untested promises 90

Reading validate.command.html and writing what it promises, without filtering by what a test could reach, produced seventeen behaviours. exhaustive then reported all seventeen as uncited. That number is the point: it is a coverage reading about intent, and the earlier pass could not produce one.

The earlier pass read twenty-eight tests and wrote twenty-eight behaviours to match, so every check passed the moment it was turned on. Coverage came out complete because coverage had been defined as whatever somebody had already tested. Running the same machinery the other way round makes the same checks say something they could not say before.

The subject earns the first turn. validate printed failures and exited zero for a week, which is the most expensive defect this repository has recorded, and nothing has tested it since. Seventeen promises with no evidence under any of them is the honest state of the thing every other check runs through.

One thing the pass settled on its own. A behaviour about the schema contract belongs to two subsystems once validate cites it as well as the schema, so the axiom it refines moved to root and eleven citations followed. The rule about where an axiom lives turned out to be readable off who cites it, rather than something anybody had to decide.
