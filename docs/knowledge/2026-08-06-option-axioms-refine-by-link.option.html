A subsystem axiom refines a general one by linking to it 75

A subsystem axiom links up to the general one it narrows, and nothing else carries the relation. No scope attribute, no subsystem vocabulary, no cascade — one more kind of edge in a corpus that is already edges.

Asking which axioms govern a subsystem becomes a walk upward from its own, which is the same walk every other question here answers with.

The cost lands on generality. A broad axiom that nobody has read down into this subsystem reaches nothing in it, and no check can tell that apart from an axiom that genuinely does not apply. Coverage of a subsystem by the general axioms stays a judgement.

That cost may be the work rather than a loss of it. The user is never surprised says nothing to anyone until somebody states what it means for a gesture engine, and a mechanism that demands the reading is a mechanism that gets it written.
