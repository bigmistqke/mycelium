An under pane means the graph stops being the page's scroll surface and becomes a region with its own. That changes arithmetic rather than layout: every wire runs between two rectangles measured against the viewport, and the drawing subtracts the grid's own rectangle to place them.

Once the grid scrolls, that rectangle is the visible box rather than the content, so each coordinate needs the scroll offset added and the drawing's canvas needs the scroll size rather than the client size.

This is what stopped the same change this morning. Nothing could catch a mistake in it: the parser computes no layout, so every rectangle measures zero and no audit sees a wire at all.

That limit closed today. A claim can carry a browser check, and one of them already drives a real page from inside a check, so the drawing's geometry can be checked the way the figure engine's already is.
