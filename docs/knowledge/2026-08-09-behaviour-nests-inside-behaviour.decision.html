Eleven test documents carry a title that groups cases inside one subsystem. The grouping is real and nothing checks it, because a document title is prose. Nesting turns each one into a claim the canon asserts and the behaviours beneath it prove.

No new type. An axiom already narrows an axiom, and reach through a narrower axiom counts says the walk goes as far as it needs. A behaviour narrowing a behaviour is that same shape one rung down.

Three rules follow. A parent behaviour carries no case, because the behaviours beneath it are its proof. A child names its parent rather than an axiom, and the reach walk finds the axiom through it. And the eleven titles have to become falsifiable claims, which some of them are not yet.

Nesting uses containment rather than an edge, since the grouping earns its place by being visible to somebody reading the document. The edge stays available beside it for a claim narrowing two others, which nothing in the corpus does today.
