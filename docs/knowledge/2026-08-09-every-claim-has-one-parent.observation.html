Every claim in the canon has exactly one parent, so the graph is a forest today 95

Counted across every canon document. Ninety-one axioms and behaviours carry exactly one depends_on, and five carry none — the root axioms and the specifications, which point at files rather than at claims. Nothing in the corpus names two parents.

That decides a question about encoding. Nesting one claim inside another gives a tree, and a tree cannot hold a claim with two parents. The corpus has no such claim, so containment loses nothing that exists.

It also says what to keep. The depends_on edge stays available beside the nesting, so a behaviour narrowing two others has somewhere to say so. The markup carries the parent a claim has, and the edges make the whole thing a directed graph again.
