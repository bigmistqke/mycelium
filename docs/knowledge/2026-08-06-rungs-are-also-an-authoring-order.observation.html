The rungs are an authoring order as much as an abstraction descent 80

The rungs descend through abstraction: an axiom, then a rule, then a behaviour, then a mechanism. They are also close to the order someone writes them in, which is what lets one structure carry two jobs. Read downward, the chain says what a system is, at whichever altitude you cut it. Read forward, it says what to do next.

The two readings do not line up exactly, and the mismatch is the useful part. The order of work runs: state the rule, stub the implementation so the types check, write the tests against that stub and watch every one fail, then fill the implementation in until they pass. The bottom rung comes twice — its shape before the tests, its body after.

So a gate cannot ask whether the rung above is finished. It has to ask a narrower question, about which part of a rung exists. A signature before a test is the method working. A body before a test is the method skipped.

That is the gate on shape move again, and it answers what rg could not hold an agent to on trust alone.
