rule and guide are the pair worth having. Their difference became structural an hour earlier, and it is the difference between something that fails the build and something you write down and do not enforce. A reader meeting either word had nowhere to look.

dictionary is the one that had actually moved. It named the whole family until the rename, so a node from before 2026-08-01 saying the dictionary may mean all three types, and one from after means the terms alone. The entry clarifies which sense each node uses.

Five entries, eight cross-links between them, all resolving. That the family had gone this long without them is the same shape as everything else found today: the thing that checks something is the last thing anyone checks.
