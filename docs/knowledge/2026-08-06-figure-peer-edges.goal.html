The one mermaid diagram left unconverted is DESIGN.html's shape of the whole thing. It reads as three tiers: instance documents, then the templates they conform to, then the scripts that validate them.

A layering by dependency does fit the engine today, and it destroys that reading: templates end up scattered across two rows and scripts across three. Keeping the tiers puts both instance documents in one row, and the leads_to edge between them then runs inside that row.

Such an edge has no channel. assignLanes works a lane out from the gap between two rows, and for one row that gap is negative, so the wire turns back through the boxes it joins. The audit skips the case and the template names it as unwritten.

Peer edges come first and on their own. Removing the mermaid diagrams waits until the figures that replace them are worth the swap.
