One word, repeatable, and the placeholder sequence is always in force 85 completed

The schema grows exactly one word. repeatable on a placeholder says the child may appear more than once, and its absence makes a second one an error. Together with required that covers the four cases: at most one, exactly one, zero or more, one or more.

The order of the placeholders is the content model, and it always holds. There is no word to relax it, because a document that does not match its declared shape is wrong the same way a missing required field is wrong.

This rejects a wrapper element. template.template.html commits to turning the existing skeleton into a schema without adding any new wrapper elements, and cardinality belongs to a placeholder rather than to a container around it.

It also rejects a word saying whether a tool may sort a run. That word gated autofix and nothing else, since the check behaved the same with or without it. Nothing in the project sorts a run, and the figure spec puts an authoring command out of scope, so it would have shipped as a permission for something that cannot happen.

Order inside a run is content rather than structure. Which task comes second is whatever the author chose; the schema says nothing about it and needs no word to stay silent. What the schema constrains is order between placeholders. That split is what makes the sorting permission unnecessary.

One question remains: a compositor that lets placeholders interleave. This decision defers it rather than rejecting it — see the option below.
