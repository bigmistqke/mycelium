Alongside the .html specs themselves, rg did real work linking axioms, spec rules, tests and implementation via structured citations (anchors), checked by a script for resolution and coverage. The project tried to hold Claude to a strict axiom->spec->test->implementation protocol, and found it hard to keep an agent following that discipline on trust alone. Making the citations structured text — rather than prose references — turned "did it follow the protocol" from something asserted into something a checker could verify: testable and accountable, not just requested.
