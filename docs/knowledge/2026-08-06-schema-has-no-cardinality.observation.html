No figure is validated at all, and duplicate fields are the corpus-wide leak 95

The figure spec's open question says the schema checks one child of each kind and ignores the rest, and that eleven plan documents lose the same way. I ran the three cases against a scratch copy of the corpus. Both halves are off.

Figures get zero checks, not one. No figure-graph in the corpus carries data-conforms-to — not the one in DESIGN.html, not the report's, not the test fixtures'. Instances come from querySelectorAll("[data-conforms-to]"), so validateFromTemplate has never run on a figure.

Setting the first node's data-at to nowhere passes, and so does setting the second's to middle. The three figure audits still fire, because they walk for figure-graph elements rather than for instances. That is why an audit still catches a bad edge endpoint, and nothing at all catches a bad placement.

Plan documents do not lose this way. Every plan-task, plan-step and plan-check carries its own data-conforms-to, so each one is a first-class instance. Breaking the required plan-files on the last task of a three-task document fails validation exactly as breaking the first does.

What plans really lose is duplicates. The field lookup is Array.from(el.children).find(…), which takes the first match. A second <plan-title> added to a step validates clean: the validator takes one title and leaves the other as dead content that no field holds and no query reaches.

That is the same failure nothing-outside-the-type guards against at the body level, one level down and unguarded, and it applies to every family rather than to plans. The gap underneath both is that the vocabulary has no cardinality. A placeholder can say a child is required, matches a pattern, or comes from an enum, but not how many times it may appear.
