Rebuild the canon through its own commands rather than migrate the files 80 completed

Moving to a document per subsystem invalidated every address in the canon, so every entry and every citation had to change. A script rewriting the files would have taken minutes. Rebuilding through canon add and canon link took about ninety invocations.

The rebuild won because it ran the new commands on every entry rather than on one. That caught two things reading them would not have: a specification cannot require a behaviour at birth, since it gets created empty and then filled, and link does have to accept a specification as a source after all, for specifies.

The cost was smaller than it looked, and worth measuring rather than guessing. A single invocation is about 1.6 seconds, so the rebuild cost roughly two and a half minutes. The session's real time went elsewhere.
