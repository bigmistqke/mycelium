A subject whose record lives outside its commits cannot test a held-out design 86

pulse holds 291,605 words of documentation against 3,523 lines of source, across 88 files. It is a documentation project with a small implementation inside it.

Holding the documents directory out therefore removes most of the record rather than isolating an answer key. The readings work from a minority channel.

Worse for the design, the agent writing those commit messages had the documents in view. So the messages compress the documents, and reading them is reading the documents once removed.

That is the concrete form of the false-agreement risk. Three lenses agreeing proves little when every channel descends from one source.
