A node's title is a link today, so following a claim means leaving the page and losing the selection. The reasoning a claim carries has nowhere to appear at all: an axiom's detail, a parent behaviour's detail and a declaration's comment are three things a reader wants beside the graph rather than instead of it.

An overlay was the first shape and the wrong one. The graph scrolls sideways by design now, so a pinned overlay covers whichever column a reader scrolled to, and one that scrolls away stops being reachable.

A pane beneath the graph avoids both. It spans the viewport, takes its own height, and covers nothing.
