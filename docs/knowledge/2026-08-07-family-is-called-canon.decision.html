The family is called canon, because the tag prefix has to name a thing 80 completed

Two types and a nested third carry a family prefix on every tag and every field, so the prefix appears far more often than the family name does in prose. That makes how it reads in a compound the deciding test.

Derivation names the relation between one rung and the next rather than the documents themselves, so derivation-axiom puts two abstractions together with no picture behind either. The same failure hits every category word tried against it: method-, practice-, process- and protocol- all leave a compound that says nothing.

A canon is the standing body of principle a work answers to, which is what these documents hold. It survives every compound, including the field names that outnumber the types: canon-title, canon-confidence, canon-detail.

Naming the documents rather than the activity is also what every family here already does. knowledge names what the graph holds and not the act of learning, and language names the vocabulary and not the writing. So the methodology stays a phrase in prose.

Rejected along the way, each for a stated reason. chain reads well but could mean almost anything. trace collides with stack traces and with tracing. protocol and discipline are already language terms with settled meanings. techne and poiesis name the making exactly and no reader would recognise either.
