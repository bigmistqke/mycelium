The knowledge graph is already a chain-of-thought schema, at the wrong grain and the wrong time 80

A goal leading to a decision, a decision holding options, an action naming what it depends on: that is a reasoning trace with every step saying what it answers to. It is persistent, addressable, and already refuses a step that connects to nothing.

Two things separate it from what a reasoning loop needs. A node gets written after a decision, so the record follows the thinking rather than shaping it. And a node is the size of a decision, while reasoning moves in steps much smaller than that.

So the structure needed already exists and runs at the wrong grain and the wrong moment. That makes the gap a harness question rather than a design one, which is the kind of question this project already answers.
