Feature-then-repair sequences recovered rationale from a region the messages left blank 84

The messages lens marked the whole speculative-scope and action feature as silent. Four commits, titles only, and no message saying why any of it was needed.

The repair lens found three constraints inside that same region. A speculative write has to invalidate every reachable downstream node rather than direct subscribers only. A speculative computed's reads belong in the scope's read set. A computed's body has to re-run on every dependency change even when the published value holds steady.

Each came from the shape of a feature commit followed closely by a fix, a regression test, or a one-line correction tagged as an open question. None of it appears in any message.

This is the first concrete evidence that reading a history several ways beats reading it once, and it cost one extra agent.
