Collapse whitespace in the generic validator, then move the title limit into the schema 90 completed

Two commits. 24fca48 makes the generic validator read a field's value with whitespace collapsed rather than merely trimmed, which was a bug on its own: a value written across indented source lines carried its newlines and leading spaces into everything that read it. The longest title in the corpus measured 139 that way against its real 135, four characters from a limit it was nowhere near. 4e55fef then replaces the title-length audit with pattern="^.{1,140}$" on each type's own title field.

The order was forced. The audit's own prose argued that a line-oriented regex silently misses every wrapped title, and 14 of the 47 titles it once caught wrapped. That objection is correct against raw text and answered by the collapse, so the fix had to land first for the rule to be expressible at all.

Verified at the boundary rather than by reading: 140 characters accepted, 141 rejected, nothing written on the rejection. Four audits are now three.

Two costs found while doing it, both recorded in the template rather than left to be discovered. The limit appeared once and now appears six times, with nothing checking that the six agree. And audits skip templates/ while per-instance validation does not, so the sample node carrying a deliberately over-long title to demonstrate the rule would have failed its own template. That fixture had to be shortened, which means the migration is not behaviour-neutral for the templates directory even though it is for the corpus.
