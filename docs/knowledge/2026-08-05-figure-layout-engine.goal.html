Make the grid smarter rather than replacing it with computed positions 72 pending

Three constants got tuned across three commits: the elbow fraction, the lane step and the side offset. Each fixed the diagram in front of it, none is a rule, and every value was fitted to one figure.

The uneven spacing had a different cause and a much smaller fix. A row is as tall as its tallest box, that box may be in any column, and boxes aligned to the top leave a pocket of slack underneath. Filling the row instead makes the distance between two boxes exactly the row gap, in every column.

So the grid stays. Absolute positions would decouple the columns and lose the thing the grid gives for free, which is that a figure reads as rows and columns rather than as boxes that happen to line up.

What remains genuinely unsolved is routing an edge that does not run straight down. The back edge still leaves a constant number of pixels to the side, and two crossing edges stay crossed. Lanes allocated in the channel between two rows would replace that, and a label would sit at its lane's midpoint, empty by construction.

Still out of scope: ports on the sides of a node, curved edges, self-loops and nested groups.
