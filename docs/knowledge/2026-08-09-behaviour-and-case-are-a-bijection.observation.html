Behaviours and cases are a bijection today: eighty of each, one citation apiece 95

Counted against the corpus rather than assumed. The canon documents declare 80 behaviours and the test documents declare 80 cases, and every case carries exactly one depends_on edge.

Three further measurements decide whether containment can replace the edge:

  • Runner kind is uniform per canon. All four figure documents are browser cases and cite figure.canon.html; every other document is node. So the rule that a document's cases share one runner survives as a per-canon property.
  • test-subject repeats what the specification above it already declares with specifies. Every test document names the file it exercises, and the specification names the same file.
  • Two audits exist only to police the joint. One checks that every behaviour has a case, the other that every case cites a behaviour. A declared child makes both unbreakable rather than checked.

The bijection is partly an artifact of retrofitting, so it is evidence that containment fits rather than a rule to freeze. Containment as a repeatable child still allows several cases per behaviour; what it forbids is one case covering two behaviours, which nothing in the corpus does.
