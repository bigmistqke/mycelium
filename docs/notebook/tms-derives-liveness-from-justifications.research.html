Truth maintenance solved derived liveness in 1979

Doyle's TMS: every belief carries its justifications. A belief is in if at least one valid justification holds, otherwise out. On a contradiction, both contradicting beliefs go out, and the system recursively propagates — anything derived from them loses that justification and goes out too, unless independently supported.

That is a more rigorous version of what this project reached for by hand. Status is not typed in; it is computed from what still supports what. Assumption-based TMS additionally holds multiple contexts of belief at once, which is the multi-membership problem (v0's query-interface decision belongs both to v0 and to the v3 synthesis leaning on it).

Applied across template families, propagation is what the unified system buys that ADRs cannot: an outcome refuting a claim can walk to the spec that relied on it and the plan derived from that spec. ADRs only link to other ADRs.

Sources: A Truth Maintenance System (Doyle, 1979); Belief Revision and Truth Maintenance Systems: An Overview.
