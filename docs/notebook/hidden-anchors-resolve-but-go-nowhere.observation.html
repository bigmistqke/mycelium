A link into a hidden element resolves and still goes nowhere

Every template keeps its worked examples inside a display:none block, so the page reads as prose rather than as a wall of fixture markup. A browser cannot scroll to a hidden element, so a link into one lands on a page that never moves.

every-link-resolves passes all of them. It asks whether the file exists and whether something carries that id, which are the two questions it answers, and neither is the question a reader has. Eight links in the corpus land inside such a block, across the plan, knowledge and canon templates.

The canon ones matter most, because the drawing links every axiom and behaviour back to where it lives and three of those live in the template's own samples. Those blocks are visible now, which is also what the prose beside them already claims: real instances rather than illustrations.

The other two templates still hide theirs, and the check still cannot tell. This is the same shape as the rest of the day: a check answering a narrower question than the one that matters, and passing confidently on the difference.
