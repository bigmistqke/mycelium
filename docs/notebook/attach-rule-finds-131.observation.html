The attach rule works and finds 131 loose comments documenting declarations

The rule fires on an inline comment whose subject names something, and passes a doc block. Running it over the repository found 131 across 18 files.

The three densest are language.template.html with 35, src/run.ts with 19, and knowledge.template.html with 15. The command written to judge comment quality holds 9 of its own.

So the observation that prompted this holds at scale. Most prose sitting above a declaration here documents that declaration, and writes itself in the one form that does not attach.

The rule sits outside the corpus for now, since a gate nobody can pass blocks every other piece of work. Converting 131 comments is mechanical — parseComment and formatAsComment already read and write both forms — but it rewrites 18 files and wants a decision first.
