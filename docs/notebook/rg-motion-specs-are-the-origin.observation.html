Mycelium's HTML-as-substrate traces to rg's motion-engine specs, not independent convergence

The idea for mycelium came directly from working on rg's gesture/camera motion engine: while writing the .html specs for that engine, they turned out to be expressive enough — self-contained, addressable, diagram-capable, dark/light themed, viewable with no build step — that the format itself suggested the project. This was not abstract reasoning about hypertext's qualities arrived at separately; the motion-engine specs were the concrete thing that prompted it.

That reframes v4 and rg's proposed ADR-0018 independently converged on the same two link-protocol moves. The shared moves (typed self-declaration, backlinks computed rather than stored) aren't parallel discovery — v4's html-as-substrate choice descends from encountering rg's specs in the first place.
