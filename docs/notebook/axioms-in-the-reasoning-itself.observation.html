The canon works as a lens and not as a gate, but only when somebody applies it before deciding. Applied after, it is a constraint with better clothes: the answer is already chosen and the axiom gets asked whether it objects.

The proposal is a layer under the harness. A harness shapes which tools get called and what comes out; this shapes the form a reasoning step may take. No step goes anywhere without naming what it derives from, the same way a declaration in a specified file names its claim or does not belong there.

The substrate is the one after training rather than inside it. Nothing here asks for different weights — it asks for the reasoning loop to carry the canon while it runs.
