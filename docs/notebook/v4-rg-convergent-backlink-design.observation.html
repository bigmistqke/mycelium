v4 and rg's proposed ADR-0018 independently converged on the same two link-protocol moves

v4's data-conforms-to/data-rel machinery and rg's proposed ADR-0018 derivation-link protocol converged independently on the same two moves:

  • typed self-declaration, and
  • backlinks computed at render, never stored.

The convergence holds despite quite different topologies: v4's graph is federated across many files, while rg's is anchors within one document per subsystem.

 informs the exploration of how v4 could serve rg's methodology
