Structural citation drift closes deterministically; hollow citations need mutation testing or blind re-derivation

Structural derivation drift — missing, broken, or dangling citations — is closable deterministically. A hollow citation is not: it needs one of two further checks.

  • Mutation testing scoped to the cited rule — deterministic, catches decorative or vacuous citations.
  • An independent blind re-derivation check by a second agent — probabilistic, catches drift within genuinely-coupled tests.
