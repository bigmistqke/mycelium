Make a test case a declared child of a behaviour, rather than a separate family reached by an edge

A behaviour becomes provable in the document that states it. The case moves inside canon-behaviour as a declared child, and the test family loses its document type, its title and its subject.

What this buys, in order of weight. The chain drops to three ranks, so every edge crosses exactly one and the layout stops implying a step that never existed. Two audits become one line of schema, which is the project's own rule that a declaration is the whole contract. And a browser canon document proves itself when a person opens it, which is the strongest reading of the claim that prose becomes a promise once something can falsify it.

What it costs. A canon document stops being scannable: 21 behaviours in the comments canon would carry 21 fixtures and 21 scripts between them, and the figure fixtures are real markup. Recovering that needs a rendering decision and a listing that prints claims alone, so it is a design problem rather than a side effect to discover later.

One case covering two behaviours becomes impossible. Nothing in the corpus does it, and a case checking two claims is a case worth splitting, on the same argument that a declaration citing six behaviours names a decomposition.
