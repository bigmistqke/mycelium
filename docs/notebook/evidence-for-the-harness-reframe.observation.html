Discipline fails and protocols work: 52% compliance against a hook that never missed

Imported from the project’s earlier decision log, frozen 2026-07-23. This is the project's founding empirical result.

Three pieces of evidence, all from one session:

  • Discipline fails. CLAUDE.md mandates --commit HEAD in bold; an agent complied 52% of the time. Instructions held in context decay.
  • Protocols work. The post-commit hook fired and was complied with instantly. The commit-msg hook rejected two commits and both were fixed. The machine holding the protocol works; the model holding it does not.
  • The decisive one was a cold bounded call — see the blind test's blind test, where a fresh claude -p with one file and no session context produced 29 propositions, 14 gaps and 8 contradictions, beating a careful hand pass.

Contested since 2026-07-30. The 52% figure was measured once, on 105 commits, in a single session, and promoted to a general law. See the 52% figure is unreplicated.
