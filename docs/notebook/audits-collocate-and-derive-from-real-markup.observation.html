Audits collocate with their family and derive from real markup, not a fixture

Imported from the project’s earlier decision log, frozen 2026-07-23. Two corrections to the just-shipped audits.

1. Location. A separate audits/knowledge-integrity.html did not earn its separation. Different scope — one instance versus the whole graph — argues for a different kind of script, not a different file, and both audits concern only knowledge-* types already bundled in one file.

Folded in, with a parallel convention: data-audits="name" plus data-touches="…" alongside the existing data-validates="#id", so an audit's scope is queryable rather than prose. An audit spanning two families would still earn its own file; neither of these is that.

2. Input. Replaced the hand-typed JS fixture with extraction from the template's own sample instances, wired with real data-rel edges. querySelectorAll even reads the six type names from the document's own template ids, rather than retyping them — a closer preview of the eventual crawler than a fixture, short of actually fetching across files.
