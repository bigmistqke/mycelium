Three tidy stories, three corrections, and the user was the gate every time

Imported from the project’s earlier decision log, frozen 2026-07-23.

Three times in one session the model told a satisfying story and then corrected it:

  • "the four packages were layers all along" — a retrofitted narrative imposed on messy history.
  • "squash destroys data, do not do it" — a .map() in a parser mistaken for a design constraint.
  • "the outcome node made a false claim" — it was precise; it had been over-read.

Each time the story was more satisfying than the evidence supported, and each time the user was the gate, not the model.

That is the failure mode a verification gate exists to catch — plausible, confident, unverified — running live in the agent designing the gate. It is the strongest argument that the gate must not itself be a model.
