Edges crossing the soft/rigid template boundary carry the epistemic weight

A data-rel edge does not care what template its target conforms to, so a soft knowledge-family node and a rigid proof-family node can link directly. The interesting part is not that they can — it is that the edge crossing that boundary is what carries the weight.

Two directions, two meanings:

  • A knowledge-decision that leads_to a proof node is a commitment. It concentrates review exactly where translation-fidelity risk lives.
  • A failing proof emitting a knowledge-observation back across the seam is a refutation. It surfaces a counterexample as fresh low-confidence material.

The consequence: the graph's confidence gradient becomes legible from topology alone, without reading any node's content.
