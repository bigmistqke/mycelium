A behaviour cites its own axiom, because a subsystem cannot derive from one

The upward edge sat on the specification, so the corpus said the figure layout engine depends on documents are the source. A specification names a subsystem, and a subsystem is a grouping of files. Nothing can contradict one, so nothing can derive one from a principle either.

The alternative was leaving it there and treating the specification's edge as shorthand for every behaviour under it. That is what made one axiom carry three specifications and read as load-bearing when it was merely vague enough to cover anything.

So the edge moved down to the claim, and grounded became two per-claim checks: a behaviour naming no axiom is freelancing, an axiom no behaviour reaches is dead. Turning it on reported all thirty-four behaviours as freelancing, which was true, and forced six axioms that actually predict something.
