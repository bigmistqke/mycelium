How a subsystem's axioms relate to the general ones

Axioms cannot all sit in one flat list. The set that shaped one subsystem says nothing about the others, and pouring every subsystem's detail into one sheet buries the few statements that govern everything.

So a general axiom has to reach a specific one, and the question is by what mechanism. Two answers, and they differ on what happens to a general axiom that nobody has read down into a given subsystem.

Refinement takes it. A subsystem axiom links up to the general one it narrows, and the relation is an edge like every other relation here. Regions would need a second vocabulary of names applied by hand, which is what link, do not tag already refused once.

The cost stays real. A broad axiom nobody has read down into a subsystem reaches nothing there, and no check separates that from an axiom that genuinely does not apply. Coverage of a subsystem by the general axioms stays a judgement, and writing the reading is the work this mechanism asks for rather than a gap in it.
