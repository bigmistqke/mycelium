the figure layout engine a figure is a document that draws itself the layout engine A node lands in the row and column its data-at names. Edges arriving at one box share a single lane. A back edge runs inside the column reserved for it. Two labelled edges into one box show both labels. A label does not cover the drop belonging to another edge. No label lands on top of a box, even where the gap is full. A label longer than its column stays inside the figure. A label with nothing to clash with keeps its own anchor. A peer edge is one level line joining the two facing sides. A peer edge pointing left leaves the left side and arrives at the right one. A peer edge's label sits above its line rather than over it. A peer edge may span a column that holds nothing in its row.
