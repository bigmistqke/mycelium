Derive whatever can be derived, because a second copy drifts from the first. 90

A hand-maintained artifact rots. Anything a machine can compute from what is already written gets computed at read time and never stored: a node's liveness from its edges, a plan's progress from its steps, the downward reach of an axiom from the upward edges beneath it.

What stays declared is what nobody can infer. Belonging is declared and liveness derived. Rejection is declared, because abandoning something is an act rather than a state.
