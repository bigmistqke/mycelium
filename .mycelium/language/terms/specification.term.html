specification noun A standing normative document stating what a system does, whose requirements carry permanent addresses that tests cite.

Spelled in full, and never shortened. This repository already uses spec for something else: spec.template.html holds the shape a design or brainstorming document takes, dated to the exploration that produced it.

The two are opposites. A design document records what someone thought on a day. A specification says what the system does now, and an edit to it changes the requirement.

Folding the design document into the plan family would free the shorter word, since a plan and its design duplicate each other three ways. That is a real migration and nobody has done it. Until somebody does, the two words live side by side and only their length tells them apart.
