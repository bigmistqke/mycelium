The criterion is arrival, and it names no destination

A practice arrives from the ground pushing back while somebody works. A principle arrives from stepping back over what has accumulated. A direction arrives from wanting the work somewhere it does not currently go.

None of those three tests mentions the canon, an axiom or a check. Sorting entries by what each could be promoted into is what the earlier draft got wrong, and grounding already carries that relation as a link an axiom writes for itself. An entry's type answers where the thought came from, which stays true whatever the project later does with it.

Provenance therefore remains the axis, and the split narrows it rather than turning it. Research came from someone else, experiment came from running something, and the three below divide what used to arrive as from thinking.
