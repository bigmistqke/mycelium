Fold the test family into canon, so a behaviour holds the case that proves it i wonder if we should fold the test-template into canon and make it as a part of behavior instead. there currently is already a strong 1:1 relation between the two, partially because we are retrofitting it. but it also makes sense: `behavior` is just prose, it becomes provable once there is a test that tests it.

A behaviour states a claim in prose and nothing in the document can falsify it. The test that checks it lives in another family, in another directory, reached by an edge that two audits exist to police. The proposal makes the case a declared child of the behaviour instead, so the claim and its falsifier are one element.

The user confirmed the direction with yes. this is the way forward. -> start with updating mycelium.knowledge, which is why this goal is active rather than pending.
