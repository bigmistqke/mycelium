Both readings are correct, and they answer different questions. A session already mid-task has something to ask about, so a rule pointing at a narrower command beats a page competing for attention. A session at its first moment has nothing to ask about yet, so there is no narrower question to point it toward.

Completeness at that one moment is what lets a question form at all. Brevity serves someone already oriented. A cold start needs orientation, and orientation is exactly what brevity cuts.
