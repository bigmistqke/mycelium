Arrival comes from git: the commit that first added a path decides the day the drawing places an entry under. An entry nobody has committed yet has no such commit, so the lookup returns nothing and the drawing opens it in a nameless column at the far left.

A one-shot drawing of an already-committed corpus never shows this seam, since by the time anyone runs it everything has a commit. A page that redraws itself while somebody works is watching the corpus mid-edit, which is exactly when an entry can exist on disk with no commit behind it yet — and that uncommitted entry is the one thing somebody redrawing the graph while they write is most likely watching for.
