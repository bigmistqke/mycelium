Raised as a goal in the earlier knowledge graph: give a template element live editor diagnostics against its own schema, flagging a missing required child or a structural problem directly in an open document rather than only when validate runs separately. In the words that raised it: like language support for template-elements and their schema, also their attributes and the like, but also their structure.

Nothing here decided how, and nothing has built it. The schema vocabulary this would diagnose against — required, repeatable, pattern, enum on a placeholder — already exists in template.template.html, so what is missing is a live reader rather than a schema to read.
